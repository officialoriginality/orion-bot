Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Orion collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Orion in a server, it may collect and store the following:

  • Discord User IDs — to identify who a moderation action was applied to and who applied it.
  • Warning records — the reason given for a warning and the moderator who issued it, stored per server and per user.
  • Server (Guild) IDs — to keep data separated between different servers Orion is used in.

Orion does not permanently store the content of your regular messages. It only reads message content as required to process commands such as ?warn @user reason and does not log or retain chat messages.

2. How We Use Information

Collected information is used only to:

  • Carry out moderation actions requested by server staff.
  • Maintain a warning history so moderators can review a member's past warnings.
  • Post moderation logs to a designated log channel within the same server, if one is configured.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties. Data collected by Orion is only accessible to:

  • Orion's operator
  • Server moderators/admins through Orion's own commands.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Warning data is stored in a local data file associated with Orion and is retained until:

  • It is cleared by a server moderator using Orion's commands.
  • Orion is removed from the server.
  • You request deletion.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect stored data, including restricting access to Orion's hosting environment and credentials. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, any personal data associated with you, such as warning records, by contacting Orion's operator directly on Discord.

7. Children's Privacy

Orion is not intended for use by anyone under the age required by Discord's own Terms of Service. We do not knowingly collect data from underage users beyond what Discord itself provides.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Orion after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Orion's operator on Discord.
